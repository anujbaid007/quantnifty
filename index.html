How it works

The Double Engine

Your shares earn the market's return. The margin locked behind those shares earns nothing until you put a second engine on it. Same capital, nothing sold, two returns instead of one.

01 You hold

The stocks work

₹1 crore in NIFTYBEES earns whatever the index earns. Historically that has averaged about 12% a year.

02 You pledge

The margin unlocks

Your broker releases trading margin against those holdings. You have not sold anything and you have not borrowed, so there is no interest to pay.

03 We deploy

The second engine runs

That idle margin funds systematic option strategies on NIFTY and SENSEX. Its return stacks on top of the first.

The number that matters

Return is loud.
Sharpe is honest.

Anyone can post a return. Take enough risk and a good month is inevitable. Sharpe asks the harder question: how much return did each unit of risk actually buy?

A Sharpe above 2 is considered excellent by institutional standards. Above 3 is rare. Apex sits at 4.67. Alpha and Whale both sit at 7.17, because they run the same signals at different sizing.

Whale

You set the drawdown.
The engine sizes itself.

Most systems hand you a return and leave the risk wherever it lands. Whale works the other way round. You name the deepest drawdown you are willing to sit through, and the position sizing is solved backwards from that.

Because sizing is the only thing that changes, the Sharpe ratio stays at 7.17 at every setting. You are moving along one risk-return line, not switching strategies.

Search at scale

A person can test twenty ideas in a week. A machine tests three million. We generate the whole parameter space, timeframe, stop level, entry window, strike offset, and run it against years of tick-level option data with real slippage and real charges.

Overfitting defence

The dangerous result is the one that looks perfect. So we rank every candidate in sample, then check whether that ranking predicts anything out of sample. On the SENSEX search it predicted nothing, rank correlation −0.001, so we picked by structure instead of by score. Most searches never run that check.

AI systems we build

Machine learning signal models, feature pipelines, regime classifiers and the research tooling around them. If the edge you want needs learning rather than fixed rules, that is a system we can build and test the same way.

The live engines stay deterministic. Every entry, stop and exit is a rule you can read in the code. AI narrows the search and stress tests what survives. It never decides, on its own, when your money moves.

Common questions

What people ask us first.

Straight answers about pledged margin, what we build, and what we are not.

How can I earn from my stock portfolio without selling it?

By pledging it. When you pledge shares or ETFs with your broker, the broker releases trading margin against their value while you keep ownership, dividends and long-term capital-gains treatment. That released margin can fund a separate options strategy, so the same capital produces two returns instead of one. Nothing is sold and nothing is borrowed.

What is pledged margin, and does it cost interest?

Pledged margin is collateral value your broker recognises against securities you already own. It is not borrowed money, so no interest is charged. Brokers apply a haircut, so ₹1 crore of holdings usually releases about ₹75 to 85 lakh of usable margin, depending on the instrument.

What is a good Sharpe ratio?

The Sharpe ratio measures return per unit of risk. Roughly speaking, a bank deposit sits near 0.3, buy-and-hold NIFTY near 0.6, a good equity mutual fund near 1.0, and a strong hedge fund near 2.0. Anything above 3 is rare. QuantNifty's backtested engines sit between 4.67 and 7.17 over their stated windows.

What does 0–1 DTE mean?

DTE means days to expiry. A 0-DTE trade is opened and closed on the contract's expiry day, and 1 DTE is the session before it. Both are intraday, so nothing is carried overnight and there is no gap risk between sessions.

The engines that go live stay deterministic: every entry, stop and exit is a fixed rule you can read in the code, so no model decides on its own when capital moves. We also build machine learning systems for clients, including signal models, feature pipelines and regime classifiers.

How do you stop an AI search from overfitting?

By testing whether the search's own ranking has any predictive power. Candidates are ranked on in-sample data, then that ranking is compared against out-of-sample results. On our SENSEX sleeve search across 3.2 million configurations the rank correlation came out at −0.001, meaning the in-sample score predicted nothing. So the final basket was picked on structure and membership frequency instead of score. Walk-forward validation, realistic slippage on both sides of every trade and full statutory charges are applied throughout.

Where it is needed, live execution as well, which means broker-side stop-losses, fill confirmation, order repricing, spike-trigger detection and a kill switch.

A single NIFTY sleeve needs roughly ₹1.6 lakh of hedged margin. What matters more than size is that the drawdown is set to something you can actually sit through.
