What the option market
says the index is worth.

Put-call parity gives a synthetic index level from option prices alone. Shown here against the actual spot, with the gap between them.

connecting
What this is

K + CE − PE gives the synthetic future, not spot. The gap against spot is the market's implied cost of carry, and that gap is the interesting number, so it is deliberately left undiscounted. Legs are priced on the mid of a two-sided quote rather than the last trade, and each figure averages the at-the-money strike with its two neighbours.
